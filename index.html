Q3 Parking Simulator

Take the driver's seat of a white Audi Q3 (left-hand drive). Top speed is 1 km/h. Reverse into the space using your mirrors and the steering wheel.

Parallel parking: reverse into the yellow space along the curb.

Square parking: reverse into the yellow bay in the parking lot.

Pick a scenario and watch it being parked from the driver's seat. Captions explain every action: when to steer, when not to steer, which pedal to use and which mirror to check.

Signal, hold the clutch and select reverse, then reverse slowly into the yellow space using the mirrors. When you're parked, apply the parking brake (P) to get your score. The steering wheel only stays turned while you keep steering: let go and it spins back to the centre by itself. It glows green when it is exactly straight.
